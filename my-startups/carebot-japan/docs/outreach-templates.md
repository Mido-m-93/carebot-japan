# CareBot Japan — Founder Outreach Templates

Internal reference for Mido's direct, founder-led outreach to English-speaking / international-patient-serving clinics in Japan (the current GTM wedge — not for SEO/content use). These are copy templates to personalize and send yourself; nothing here gets sent automatically.

**Grounded in the live product** (carebot-japan-web.vercel.app / pricing page) as of this writing:
- Patients submit a booking request in plain Japanese or English via a **web form or LINE**.
- AI reads the request, extracts name/date/time/reason, and **auto-books** the appointment — no manual data entry.
- Anything the AI isn't confident about goes to a **review queue** for one-click staff confirmation.
- **Live dashboard** shows today's/this week's appointments and pending reviews at a glance.
- Fully **bilingual** — patients and staff can each use whichever language they prefer.
- Pricing: Starter is free (up to 50 appointments/month); **Pro is ¥7,500/month** (unlimited appointments, priority support); Enterprise is ¥15,000/month (multi-location).
- The design-partner offer: **free Pro access for 3-6 months** in exchange for feedback + eventual testimonial/case study.

## 1. About CareBot Japan (elevator pitch blurb)

Drop this paragraph into any outreach, LinkedIn message, or intro call as needed:

> CareBot Japan is an AI-powered appointment scheduling assistant built for clinics in Japan. Patients submit a booking request in plain Japanese or English — through a simple web form or LINE, the messaging app nearly every patient in Japan already has installed — and CareBot's AI reads the request, pulls out the name, date, time, and reason for the visit, and confirms the appointment automatically. Anything the AI isn't fully confident about is routed to a review queue so staff stay in control with a single click, instead of re-typing every request by hand.

**Concrete benefits for a clinic serving international/English-speaking patients:**

- **Patients book in their own words, in their own language.** English-speaking patients no longer have to fight through a Japanese-only phone tree or booking form to get an appointment — they just type what they need.
- **Your front desk isn't stuck translating.** A request submitted in English still lands correctly on the same Japanese-language dashboard your staff already use — no bilingual staff member has to be free to take the call.
- **It works over LINE, not just a web link.** Since most patients in Japan already have LINE, there's no new app to download or account to create for them to reach you.
- **Nothing gets booked by mistake.** If the AI isn't sure about a request, it waits in a review queue for a staff click instead of auto-confirming — so ambiguous or unusual requests never silently fall through the cracks.

## Usage notes

- Personalize the pain-point sentence with something specific if Mido knows it (e.g., "I noticed your site only has a Japanese booking form" or "a patient mentioned they had trouble booking by phone").
- Keep the ask singular — either the call *or* the demo link, not both framed as required steps.
- If a clinic replies positively, the natural next step is either the 15-minute call or sending them straight to `/signup` on the live site so they can start the free Starter tier immediately, with Pro access applied manually afterward for design partners.
