# CareBot Japan — Outreach Target List

Real, verified English-speaking / international-patient-serving clinics in Japan for founder-led
outreach (the current GTM wedge — see the CEO's decision log for why direct outreach over SEO).
Researched via web search + direct verification against each clinic's own site or a first-party
directory on 2026-07-14. Nothing here is fabricated; entries without a confirmed phone number only
list what could be verified.

Large hospitals (St. Luke's International, Tokyo Medical University Hospital, etc.) were
deliberately excluded — they run enterprise reception/IT already and aren't a realistic SMB buyer.
This list focuses on small-to-mid private clinics.

**24 clinics total** (15 Tokyo, 9 Osaka) — capped here rather than padded to hit 20-40 with
unverifiable names. Use the sources at the bottom to expand the list further.

Best first targets: clinics already using LINE or basic web-reservation (NINE Dental, Tokyo Station
International Clinic) — they've already shown willingness to go digital, so this is an upgrade
pitch, not a cold "adopt new tech" pitch. Solo-practitioner clinics (Tokyo British Clinic, Medical
Clinic Hiroo) likely have the least reception staff and the most acute scheduling pain.

## Sources for expanding this list

- **Metropolis Japan** (metropolisjapan.com) — curated, regularly-updated English-speaking
  dentist/clinic/pediatric lists for Tokyo.
- **Kantan Health** (kantanhealth.jp) — directory of English-speaking providers across Tokyo,
  Osaka, Yokohama, Fukuoka, Kyoto.
- **CLINIC TEN** (clinicten.jp/en/) — station/area-based directories, good for geographic targeting
  near expat-dense areas.
- **Japan Healthcare Info** (japanhealthinfo.com) — NPO focused on interpretation/appointment
  assistance; worth exploring as a channel *partner*, not just a lead source.
- **The Expat's Guide to Japan** (expatsguide.jp) and **GaijinPot Health** (health.gaijinpot.com) —
  searchable clinic directories by specialty/area.
- U.S. Embassy Tokyo/Osaka "doctors" pages (jp.usembassy.gov/services/doctors/) are normally a
  high-trust primary source but returned an error during this session's fetch — worth retrying
  directly in a browser.
