## Day 1 — Arrival & Shinjuku

**Theme: Land, Orient, Breathe**

### Morning / Afternoon (Arrival)
- [ ] Clear customs and immigration at Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND)
- [ ] Pick up Pocket WiFi / activate SIM at airport counter
- [ ] Load Suica card at airport kiosk
- [ ] Take Narita Express (N'EX) or Keikyu Line to central Tokyo
- [ ] Check into hotel — leave bags, freshen up

### Evening
- [ ] Walk around **Shinjuku** neighborhood to orient
- [ ] Dinner at Shinjuku's Memory Lane (Omoide Yokocho) — tiny yakitori stalls, casual, cheap
- [ ] Optional: convenience store (7-Eleven / FamilyMart) run for snacks and drinks — a true Tokyo ritual

> **Kanso Note:** Resist the urge to pack Day 1 with sightseeing. Jet lag is real. Orient, eat well, sleep early.

## Day 2 — Traditional Tokyo: Asakusa & Ueno

**Theme: Old Tokyo, Temples, Museums**

### Morning
- [ ] **Senso-ji Temple** (Asakusa) — arrive by 8am to beat crowds
  - Walk Nakamise-dori shopping street
  - Buy a fortune slip (omikuji) — ¥100
- [ ] **Asakusa Shrine** — adjacent, quieter, beautiful
- [ ] Breakfast at a local kissaten (old-school coffee shop) — look for ones open before 9am

### Afternoon
- [ ] Walk or taxi to **Ueno Park**
- [ ] Choose one museum (don't try all):
  - Tokyo National Museum (history, art) — ¥1,000
  - National Museum of Nature and Science — ¥630
  - Or simply walk the park if museum-fatigue hits
- [ ] **Ameyoko Market** for street food lunch — try taiyaki (fish-shaped cake with filling)

### Evening
- [ ] Dinner in Ueno or return to Shinjuku area
- [ ] Early night — big days ahead

> **Budget estimate:** ¥3,000–¥6,000 (transport + entry + meals)

## Day 3 — Harajuku, Omotesando & Shibuya

**Theme: Fashion, Design, Crossing**

### Morning
- [ ] **Meiji Jingu Shrine** — peaceful forest shrine in central Tokyo
  - Free entry, opens at sunrise
  - Allow 45–60 minutes for the walk through the forest path
- [ ] **Harajuku** (Takeshita Street) — colorful, chaotic, fun
  - Try a crepe from Marion Crepes (the original, since 1976)

### Afternoon
- [ ] Walk **Omotesando** — Tokyo's Champs-Elysees, but calmer
  - Window-shop the architecture (Prada, Louis Vuitton buildings are design landmarks)
  - Side streets hide excellent coffee shops and boutiques
- [ ] Lunch on Omotesando or Cat Street (parallel side street)

### Evening
- [ ] **Shibuya Crossing** at dusk or night — stand on the second-floor Starbucks for the view
- [ ] Dinner in Shibuya (budget: Ichiran ramen, splurge: Gonpachi)
- [ ] Optional: **Shibuya Sky** observation deck (book online — ¥2,000)

> **Kanso Note:** Omotesando rewards slow walking. One good coffee and one good browse beats rushing through ten stores.

## Day 4 — Akihabara & Yanaka

**Theme: Electric City + Old Shitamachi**

### Morning
- [ ] **Yanaka** neighborhood — Tokyo's best-preserved old town
  - Yanaka Cemetery walk (peaceful, historic)
  - Yanaka Ginza shotengai (shopping street) — buy sembei (rice crackers) fresh
  - This area resists tourism — that's the point

### Afternoon
- [ ] **Akihabara** — electronics, anime, manga
  - Even if not into anime: Yodobashi Camera multi-floor electronics store is worth seeing
  - Retro game stores on upper floors of side-street buildings
  - Try a maid cafe if curious (Maidreamin has English staff)

### Evening
- [ ] Dinner near Akihabara or Kanda
- [ ] Early return — Day 5 is a long one

> **Budget estimate:** ¥4,000–¥8,000 (transport + shopping if any + meals)

## Day 5 — Day Trip: Nikko or Kamakura

**Theme: Escape the City (Pick One)**

### Option A: Nikko (2.5 hours each way)
- [ ] Take the Tobu Nikko Line from Asakusa (¥2,780 round trip)
- [ ] **Tosho-gu Shrine** — ornate, UNESCO-listed, famous "see no evil" monkeys
- [ ] **Shinkyo Bridge** — iconic lacquered red bridge
- [ ] Lunch: tofu cuisine (Nikko specialty) at a local restaurant
- [ ] Return by 6pm — long travel day

### Option B: Kamakura (1 hour each way)
- [ ] Take JR Yokosuka Line from Tokyo Station (¥920 round trip)
- [ ] **Kotoku-in** — the Great Buddha (Daibutsu) — ¥300 entry
- [ ] **Hase-dera Temple** — beautiful hillside temple with sea views
- [ ] Walk **Komachi-dori** for lunch and shopping
- [ ] Optional: rent bikes to explore

> **Kanso Note:** Don't try both. One place, done well, beats two places done poorly.

## Day 6 — Ginza, Tsukiji & Tokyo Tower

**Theme: Upscale, Seafood, Icons**

### Morning
- [ ] **Tsukiji Outer Market** — the outer market (tourist-friendly, still great)
  - Fresh sushi breakfast — arrive by 8am for best selection
  - Try: maguro (tuna) sushi, tamagoyaki (egg), fresh oysters
  - Budget: ¥1,500–¥3,000

### Afternoon
- [ ] **Ginza** — Tokyo's most refined shopping district
  - Walk Chuo-dori (main avenue)
  - Itoya stationery store (9 floors of beautiful paper and pens)
  - Free: Tokyo International Forum (stunning architecture)
- [ ] **Hamarikyu Gardens** — traditional garden with teahouse, ¥300

### Evening
- [ ] **Tokyo Tower** at dusk (¥1,200) — the view of Mount Fuji on clear days
- [ ] Dinner in Roppongi or Azabu-Juban neighborhood
- [ ] Optional: Roppongi for nightlife (lively international crowd)

---

## Day 7 — Shinjuku & Departure

**Theme: Final Morning, Last Tastes**

### Morning
- [ ] **Shinjuku Gyoen** — Tokyo's finest garden (¥500)
  - Cherry blossom trees, French formal garden, Japanese landscape garden
  - Perfect for a slow final morning
- [ ] Last convenience store run — stock up on omiyage (souvenirs): KitKat regional flavors, regional snacks

### Afternoon (allow 4+ hours before flight)
- [ ] Head to airport — allow plenty of time
  - Narita: take N'EX from Shinjuku (85 min)
  - Haneda: take Keikyu or monorail (30–40 min)
- [ ] Duty-free shopping at airport (sake, cosmetics, snacks)

> **Kanso Note:** The best souvenir is a clear memory. Don't overfill the last day.
