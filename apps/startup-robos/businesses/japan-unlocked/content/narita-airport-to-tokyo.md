# How to Get from Narita Airport to Tokyo (All Options)

Narita Airport is not in Tokyo. That's the first thing most visitors learn the hard way — often while staring at a taxi queue and calculating the fare in their head. The airport sits about 60 kilometers from central Tokyo, and depending on how you travel, that distance costs anywhere from ¥1,000 to ¥25,000 and takes between 35 minutes and two hours.

Here is every real option, ranked by value.

---

## The Narita Express (N'EX) — Fastest, Most Convenient

The Narita Express is the default recommendation for most travelers, and it earns that status. Operated by JR East, it runs directly from Narita Terminal 1, 2/3, and connects to Shibuya, Shinjuku, Ikebukuro, and Yokohama — not just Tokyo Station.

**Cost:** Around ¥3,070 one-way to Tokyo Station. Round-trip tickets (¥4,070) are a strong deal if you're returning to Narita.

**Travel time:** 53 minutes to Tokyo Station.

**Best for:** Anyone with a JR Pass, or travelers landing at Terminal 1 or 2 who want a guaranteed seat without hustling.

If you're booking in advance, Klook often offers N'EX tickets at a discount with no queue at the machine: [https://www.klook.com/en-US/search/?query=narita+express+tokyo]. Worth checking before you fly.

One practical note: the N'EX does not run 24 hours. Last trains wrap up around 21:30 from the airport. If you're landing late, keep reading.

---

## Keisei Skyliner — Cheaper and Nearly as Fast

The Keisei Skyliner is the smarter choice for anyone staying in Asakusa, Ueno, or east-side Tokyo. It runs from Narita to Nippori and Ueno stations, which puts you closer to many budget hotels and traditional neighborhoods than the N'EX does.

**Cost:** ¥2,570 one-way.

**Travel time:** 41 minutes to Nippori, 51 minutes to Ueno.

**Best for:** East Tokyo destinations, budget-conscious travelers who still want speed.

The Keisei Skyliner consistently beats the N'EX on both price and travel time to Nippori. The only downside is coverage — if you're heading to Shinjuku or Shibuya, you'll need to transfer.

You can book Keisei Skyliner tickets through Klook with a combo pass that includes a 24-hour Tokyo subway ticket: [https://www.klook.com/en-US/country/9-japan-things-to-do/_KEISEI]. That combination is hard to beat for the first day.

---

## Airport Limousine Bus — Best If You're Staying Near a Major Hotel

The Airport Limousine Bus (not a limousine in the Western sense — just a comfortable coach) runs direct routes to major hotels and train stations across Tokyo. No transfers. You load your luggage underneath and ride.

**Cost:** ¥1,000–¥3,200 depending on destination.

**Travel time:** 75–120 minutes, entirely dependent on traffic.

**Best for:** Travelers with heavy bags, families, or anyone staying near Shinjuku Station, Tokyo Station, or a major hotel hub.

The honest caveat: Tokyo traffic is real. I've done the limousine bus in 80 minutes on a quiet Tuesday and sat through 140 minutes on a Friday evening. If you have a time-sensitive connection, pick the train.

---

## Taxi or Private Transfer — When It Actually Makes Sense

A standard taxi from Narita to central Tokyo runs ¥20,000–¥25,000. That number ends most conversations.

But private transfers are worth considering for groups of three or four splitting the cost, late arrivals after trains stop, or business travelers who need door-to-door service without thinking.

Viator has vetted private airport transfer options that include meet-and-greet service at arrivals: [https://www.viator.com/Japan/d660-ttd_TRANSFER]. For solo travelers or couples, skip it. For a family of four with jetlag and luggage, the math starts to work.

| Option | Cost | Time | Best For |

## FAQ

<!-- FAQ_SCHEMA_START -->

**Q: How long does it take to get from Narita Airport to Tokyo?**
A: The fastest option is the Keisei Skyliner at 41 minutes to Nippori. The Narita Express takes about 53 minutes to Tokyo Station. The limousine bus ranges from 75 to 120 minutes depending on traffic.

**Q: What is the cheapest way to get from Narita to Tokyo?**
A: The cheapest reliable option is the Keisei Limited Express (not the Skyliner) at around ¥1,050, which takes about 80 minutes with a transfer. If you want speed without spending much, the Keisei Skyliner at ¥2,570 is the best value balance.

**Q: Can I use my JR Pass on the Narita Express?**
A: Yes. A valid JR Pass covers the full Narita Express fare. You'll still need to reserve a seat, which you can do at JR ticket counters in the arrivals hall.
