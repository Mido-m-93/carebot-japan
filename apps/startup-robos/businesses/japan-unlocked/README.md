---

## Site Concept

JapanUnlocked publishes practical, experience-based guides for people planning trips to Japan or moving there long-term. Articles cover transportation, connectivity, accommodation, day trips, culture logistics, and money. Every article is written to answer a real question — no filler, no fluff.

The site targets English speakers from the US, UK, Australia, Canada, and Southeast Asia who are in the research phase of planning a Japan trip. Secondary audience: people already in Japan (expats, long-term visitors) who need reliable logistical how-to content.

## SEO Strategy

- **Primary KW difficulty target:** Under 25 (Ahrefs KD)
- **Content length:** 800–1,400 words for how-to articles; 1,500–2,000 for comparison pieces
- **Internal linking:** Link articles to each other (e.g., eSIM article links to Narita arrival guide)
- **Schema markup:** FAQ schema on every article
- **Publish cadence:** 2 articles per week during ramp-up phase
